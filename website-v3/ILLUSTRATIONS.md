# Showcase illustrations

The cartoon images are fictional AI-generated humour, not images of a physical trial or photographs of the founders. Captions are editable text in `public/presentation/content.js`, not baked into the images.

## New images

Six images were created with the built-in image generation tool using the spy-avocado illustration from the 26 October Investigation humour deck as a style reference. Each image used this shared prompt, followed by the relevant scene below:

> Use case: illustration-story. Create a brand-new single fictional character illustration for a ClevaCado presentation. Style reference is the supplied existing avocado illustration: vintage editorial cartoon, expressive ink outlines, halftone texture, warm green and ochre palette against an extremely dark green/black background, vivid rich lighting. New scene: [scene below] Portrait composition, character comfortably fills frame. No written words, logos, captions, watermarks or branding. This is fictional humour, not a technical diagram or experimental evidence. Do not simply reproduce the reference character's pose or costume.

- `sad.6338200fd336.jpg`: A very sad anthropomorphic avocado half, big watery eyes, a small bruise visible on the flesh, holding a tiny handkerchief. Gentle humorous pathos; never grotesque.
- `founders.bf12c36b8524.jpg`: Two cheerful anthropomorphic avocados standing together like entrepreneurial partners; one holds a small circuit board and the other holds a phone, friendly teamwork, no resemblance to real people.
- `idea.611d45a5dc23.jpg`: A curious anthropomorphic avocado with a pencil and engineering notebook, looking up at a small glowing light bulb, expression of discovery.
- `pitch.7619263a8b06.jpg`: An earnest anthropomorphic avocado standing behind a modest presentation lectern with a tiny note card and a shy but hopeful smile, no trophy, celebratory but humble.
- `pilot.b070d67cc63b.jpg`: An optimistic anthropomorphic avocado wearing a small field researcher hat, holding a map with a simple pin icon, ready to explore an orchard and supply chain, not an actual GPS screen.
- `ai.d8b87ffaf043.jpg`: A witty anthropomorphic avocado wearing round glasses, at a desk with a tiny generic robot helper and a pencil, smiling with an inquisitive expression, teamwork and engineering judgment.

The original generated PNGs are retained under the session's Codex generated-images folder. The JPEGs above are resized, compressed copies in `public/presentation/assets/illustrations/`.
