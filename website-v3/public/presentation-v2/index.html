THE PROBLEM

Damage appears later.
Its cause is hard to trace.

Losses affect growers, workers and the families who depend on the industry.

HandlingOrchard → packhouse → cold storage → transport
The blind spotDrops, vibration and compression can cause damage that only becomes visible later.
Limited visibilityTemperature logs show cold-chain conditions, not how fruit was handled.
R7.4bnSA industry estimate · 20–40% value at risk · R2.2bn midpoint
