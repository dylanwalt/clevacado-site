# ClevaCado founder presentation — speaker notes

The public presentation is at `/presentation`. The screen contains one idea at a time; use the notes below to expand on it. Suggested speaking time is approximately **6:15**, leaving about 45 seconds for transitions within a seven-minute limit. This is a planning estimate and has not been rehearsed.

1. **Two founders, one purpose — 15 sec.** Introduce Dylan Walt and Dylan Simpson and the shared desire to build something useful.
2. **Dylan Walt — 25 sec.** Early sales experience with CSX concussion software, Prevent smart mouthguards and SportGate. An earlier fintech platform was sold to Harwood Labs in early third year; that experience sparked Velzee, based at the Roux Institute. The earlier platform is the one identified as sold in the founder's earlier clarification.
3. **Dylan Simpson — 25 sec.** Approximately five years running an exotic-animal business, buying, selling, trading and breeding animals. More than 1,000 customers across Africa. Discuss sales, marketing, education and customer service.
4. **A project worth continuing — 30 sec.** At the beginning of 2026, choosing an Investigation project led to a shared goal: critical thinking, stepping outside the comfort zone, solving a real problem, and creating something to continue after graduation.
5. **Agriculture — 20 sec.** Walt suggested agriculture; their research led the team to avocados and quality losses that affect the industry and its people.
6. **We picked up the phone — 35 sec.** The team were not growers or exporters, so they cold-called industry contacts, including Fremax Farms. Some calls led to referrals. The feedback highlighted the need for visibility and accountability, and some contacts encouraged the team to return with a product.
7. **Damage can appear later — 35 sec.** Drops, vibration, compression and handling can damage fruit, while bruising may be visible later. It is difficult to establish the specific event or stage after the fact. Existing handling studies provide context; do not claim the exact cause is always unknowable.
8. **15,000 jobs — 20 sec.** The South African government reported 15,000 people employed on farms and in packhouses in 2023; many others and their families are connected to the value chain. Source: https://www.gov.za/news/media-statements/minister-thoko-didiza-signs-agreement-export-avocado-china-22-aug-2023
9. **A smart avocado — 35 sec.** Show the provisional proxy shell and describe the intended journey alongside fruit. The shell separates automatically and the on-screen control can close or reopen it. The 3D model is provisional, with no electronics shown.
10. **Motion into insight — 35 sec.** The electronics record motion; the machine-learning work classifies controlled handling patterns. The displayed signal is one controlled nominal 15 cm drop. Proxy motion is not a bruise measurement; linking events to fruit outcomes requires validation.
11. **2nd at Wits — 25 sec.** The founders report second place at Wits EDHE and representing Wits at the regional round. Mentoring and feedback informed the business case. Do not imply a regional win.
12. **Learning by doing — 20 sec.** Discuss mentorship and the Wits–AfreTec Challenge as experiences that helped the team see how engineering and AI can address real problems across Africa. The photo is a general project photo, not a claim about the depicted event.
13. **Eight weeks, one prototype — 30 sec.** Research and conversations shaped the eight-week Investigation. The team built sensing hardware, recorded controlled data and explored machine learning. TRL 4 is the team's assessment, not independent certification.
14. **Sharing our work — 20 sec.** Mention Wits EIE Open Day and submission of a SAUPEC paper for review. Acceptance or publication is not established.
15. **Next: learn in the field — 25 sec.** Improve device reliability, meet industry contacts and see the supply chain, collect field data, retrain the model and work towards an initial pilot. Share results with Sir John afterwards. Other delicate produce and stone fruit are future possibilities. The pilot has not been completed.
