# ClevaCado showcase — presenter notes

The user-supplied `ClevaCadoContent.pdf` provides five sections. This visual edition spreads them over 15 short screens; the notes retain the fuller story. The suggested allocations total **6:30**, leaving 30 seconds for transitions within seven minutes. This is a planning estimate, not a rehearsed timing. Let audiences read the fictional captions without reading each one aloud.

1. **Two founders — 40 sec.** Walt gained early sales experience with CSX concussion software, Prevent smart mouthguards and SportGate. An earlier fintech platform was sold to Harwood Labs in early third year; that experience sparked Velzee at the Roux Institute. Simpson spent roughly five years buying, selling, trading and breeding tarantulas, snakes and other exotic animals, building 1,000+ customers across Africa through marketing, product knowledge, education and service. Together they co-founded ClevaCado.
2. **Perfect outside, bruised inside — 20 sec.** Avocados can look fine outside while bruising is only discovered after cutting. By then the handling event can be difficult to trace. Both visuals are fictional AI-generated illustrations, not a photograph of test fruit.
3. **More than a tick-box project — 25 sec.** At the start of 2026, choose a second-semester Investigation project worth continuing after graduation. Critical thinking, stepping outside comfort zones and using engineering for real people mattered. Walt suggested agriculture; shared research led to avocados.
4. **We picked up the phone — 30 sec.** We were not growers or exporters, so we cold-called farmers and exporters across South Africa, including Fremax Farms, to validate the need. Conversations and referrals highlighted visibility and accountability. Some contacts encouraged us to return with something to show.
5. **The problem reaches people — 20 sec.** Quality losses affect growers, workers and families dependent on the industry. Contacts also highlighted similar challenges for delicate produce and stone fruit.
6. **A long journey, a blind spot — 25 sec.** Bruising can follow drops, vibration or compression during the orchard, packhouse, cold storage and transport journey. It may appear later, making the responsible event hard to trace. Temperature logging records cold-chain conditions, not necessarily handling.
7. **A costly quality problem — 20 sec.** The founders supplied the R7.4 billion sector estimate and 20–40% value-at-risk estimate. Their source has not been independently verified. The range applied to R7.4 billion is R1.48–2.96 billion; R2.2 billion is approximately the midpoint. These are not established measured losses.
8. **A smart avocado in the crate — 30 sec.** The 3D-printed proxy is designed to travel alongside fruit and record acceleration. The interactive model shows the R4 shell and locked cradle plus a photo-recreated electronics illustration. The components are test/first-print candidates, and the pictured electronics placement has not been validated. TRL 4 is the team's assessment.
9. **Six events, one learning problem — 30 sec.** The controlled tests included rubber mat, tar and dirt vibration surfaces, and nominal 15, 45 and 80 cm drops. The model classifies controlled proxy-motion recordings; actual release heights were not independently measured. Cycling waveforms are individual controlled examples, not live telemetry, direct bruise measurements or proof of field performance.
10. **EDHE — 25 sec.** After proposing the topic and before the Investigation began, we entered EDHE Absa Entrepreneurship Inno-Varsity to explore the business side. We placed second in Innovative Industries at Wits and represented Wits regionally; feedback and mentoring were valuable.
11. **Wits–Afretec — 25 sec.** The founders report being hand-selected for the challenge with Carnegie Mellon University–Africa. Walt competed in energy and Simpson in agriculture; Simpson's team won. It strengthened their confidence in applying AI to meaningful African problems.
12. **Investigation — 25 sec.** Concluded on Monday 26 October 2026, according to the user-supplied briefing's “past Monday” in its 28 October context. The eight-week Investigation assembled the components, developed the hardware and software, and collected controlled lab data to evaluate them.
13. **Incubation — 20 sec.** The founders are looking to incubate through the DSL lab, improve hardware and software, and use AI as a tool. Incubation remains an intention.
14. **Learn from the real supply chain — 30 sec.** See the chain directly, work towards a pilot, collect real supply-chain data and retrain the model. Future ambitions include live GPS, conditions such as port temperature, and better understanding of where mishandling and potential bruising occur; these are not completed capabilities.
15. **AI and judgement — 25 sec.** AI can help refine hardware and software and tailor the system to real operations, while the founders retain engineering decisions and judgement.
